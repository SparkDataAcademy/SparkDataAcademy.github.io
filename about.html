Meet the Founder & Our Network

M Madhuri Srilakshmi — Founder & Director

M Madhuri Srilakshmi is the visionary Founder of Spark Data Academy. With a deep passion for empowering the next generation of tech professionals, she founded the academy to bridge the gap between academic theory and real-world engineering challenges.

Under her leadership, Spark Data Academy operates on a "Industry-First" model. Instead of relying on traditional academic curricula, she curates and collaborates with a network of elite, active Senior Data Architects and Lead Engineers currently working in Top Tech companies and global enterprises. This ensures that every program, assignment, and capstone project reflects live production environments.

Our specialized training network covers cutting-edge technologies and modern data ecosystems:

  • Big Data Frameworks: Apache Spark, PySpark, and Distributed Computing
  • Modern Lakehouse Ecosystems: Azure Databricks, Delta Lake, and Medallion Architecture
  • Analytics Engineering: dbt-core, data quality, and Data Contracts
  • Next-Gen Data Ops: Apache Airflow orchestration, CI/CD, and Production Pipeline Automation
  • Cloud Architecture: Enterprise Lakehouse deployment strategies on Microsoft Azure

By bringing real-world practitioners directly to the students, Madhuri ensures that Spark Data Academy delivers highly practical, hands-on, and industry-aligned knowledge that truly prepares professionals for modern data roles.

Languages Supported: Telugu, English
